# What’s New in TimeVault 1.0.5

- The app icon now aligns with the new Time Machine icon introduced in macOS 27.
- Custom-named Time Machine destinations are recognized from their configured mount points instead of relying on a literal volume name.
- Approved backup-volume access and security-scoped bookmarks are revalidated more reliably when the app launches or volumes change.
- Navigation, settings, and snapshot selection are locked while discovery or comparison work is running, while comparison cancellation remains available.
- Comparison progress becomes determinate once TimeVault is actively comparing snapshot paths.
